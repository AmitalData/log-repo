How do I manage AWB stock?

Logitude allows you to manage air waybill stocks by airline, for your Air Export shipments. You can enter into the system your allocation of AWB numbers from airlines, and use a number from an airline’s stock when assigning an AWB number to a shipment.

You can remove an air waybill number from a shipment and return it to the airline’s stock.

 

Enter a series of air waybill numbers

1.   From the Logitude homepage menu, select Getting Started. Then, In Data Management, Under Master Data, select Airlines.

2.   In the Airlines page, click to open the airline for which you want to enter air waybill numbers.

3.   In the navigation pane on the left, click the Stock tab of the airline.

4.   Click Add to open the New Air Waybill Numbers dialog box.

5.   In Start Number, enter the number of the first air waybill in the stack.

6.   Do one of the following:

  • Make sure the By End Number option is selected. In End Number, enter the last number in the series. The Air Waybill Numbers area will show each one of the numbers in the given range. The Amount field will show the quantity.
  • Select the By Amount option. In Amount, enter the number of air waybills in the series. The Air Waybill Numbers area will show each one of the numbers according to the amount. The End Number field will show the number of the last air waybill in the series.

7.   Click OK. The numbers will now appear in the Stock tab together with the insertion date and time.

 

Remove an air waybill number from an airline’s stock

1.     Go to the Stock tab of the airline (as in Steps 1-3 above).

2.     In the air waybill numbers area, click on the line you want to remove and click the Remove button.

3.     Click Delete to confirm.

 

Remove an air waybill series from an airline’s stock

1.     Go to the Stock tab of the airline (as in Steps 1-3 above).

2.     Click on the air waybill numbers area to enable the Remove buttons.

3.     Click the Remove Series button. This action will delete the oldest series entered for the current airline. A confirmation message will show the insertion date of the series.

4.     Click Delete to confirm.

 

Assign an AWB number to a shipment

1.     In the Shipments list, click to open the shipment to which you want to assign an AWB number.

2.     Go to the Routings tab. Click the Edit button (pen icon) of the Main Carriage Leg.

3.     In MAWB, click the Get button and select an air waybill number from the airline’s stock. Click OK. The selected number will be removed from the stock.

 

Return an AWB number to an airline’s stock

1.     In the Shipments list, click to open the shipment from which you want to remove the AWB number.

2.     Go to the Routings tab. Click the Edit button (pen icon) of the Main Carriage Leg.

3.     In MAWB, click the X button (next to the Get button). The number will be removed from the MAWB field. In the airline’s stock the number will appear with a note saying that it has been returned to stock.
