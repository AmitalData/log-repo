How can I change the units of measurement in a shipment or a quote?

The default units of measurement for volume, dimensions and weight are defined in Application Settings (Getting Started > Customize Logitude > System Defaults). So, when you enter order and package details for a shipment or a quote, the values are in default units. You can change the units of measurement for a specific shipment or quote.

 

Change measurement settings in a shipment

1.     In the Shipments list, click to open the shipment whose measurement units you want to change.

2.     To change the measurement settings for the order packages, go to the Order tab (in the navigation pane on the left, click on Order). To change the measurement settings for the actual packages, go to the Packages tab.

3.     Click the Measurement Settings icon.

4.     In the measurement unit fields that appear at the top, open the dropdown list and select the relevant unit. The new unit(s) you have selected appear in the package details.

You can also change the default volume weight Ratio.

You can hide the measurement unit fields by clicking the Measurement Settings icon again.

5.     Click Save.

 

Change measurement settings in a quote

1.     In the Quotes list, click to open the quote whose measurement units you want to change.

2.     In the Details tab, click the Measurement Settings icon.

3.     In the measurement unit fields that appear at the top, open the dropdown list and select the relevant unit. The new unit(s) you have selected appear in the Expected Order Details.

You can also change the default volume weight Ratio.

You can hide the measurement unit fields by clicking the Measurement Settings icon again.

4.     Click Save.
